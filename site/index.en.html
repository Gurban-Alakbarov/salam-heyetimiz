QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

Clear answers to your questions.

About how the service works, installation and use.

What is Salam Sesame?+

Salam Sesame is a service that lets you control barriers, garage doors and compatible automatic entrance gates through a mobile app.

Which access systems can it be used with?+

It can be connected to existing barriers, garage doors, automatic gates and access systems that are technically compatible. Compatibility is checked after you submit a request.

How is installation carried out?+

Upon request, technical compatibility is checked and a suitable device is installed on the existing access system.

How is the price of the service determined?+

The price is determined individually after your request and our discussion, based on the device type, the characteristics of the facility and the agreed service terms.

How long is the subscription?+

Salam Sesame is offered on an annual subscription. Current payment terms are shown in the mobile app.

Can I add my family members?+

Yes. An authorized subscriber can invite their family members to give them access permission.

Can I create access for a guest?+

Yes. A user with the appropriate access rights can create one-time or time-limited guest links.

What can a building manager do?+

Within their complex, a building manager can manage invitations and access rights of residents in line with their authority.

Can I control the gate without internet?+

Sending commands from the mobile app depends on a network connection. Without internet or a device connection, a command may be delayed or may not be executed.

What happens if I stop my subscription renewal?+

When auto-renewal is stopped, access continues until the end of the subscription period already paid for.

How can I manage my account?+

For account management and requests about personal data, you can use the support section.

1. General information

This Privacy Policy explains how data is processed when you use the mobile app and related website services provided by Alkin Group MMC under the Salam Sesame brand. The Company processes personal data for the purposes of providing the service, ensuring security and fulfilling its legal obligations.

2. Data collected

Depending on the nature of the service, the following data may be processed:

  • First name, last name, e-mail address and phone number;
  • Information about registration, account verification and user role;
  • Residential complex, connected devices, access permissions and invitations;
  • History of device commands, access attempts and related security events;
  • Records related to subscriptions, payment status and requests;
  • Technical identifiers required to deliver notifications;
  • Organization details and facility location provided in requests from legal entities.

3. Location data

Salam Sesame does not continuously track the live location of users. However, when a distance-based security restriction is enabled on certain access devices, the current location of the user may be required at the moment an access command is sent. This data is used only with permission and within the scope of the relevant feature.

4. Purposes of data use

  • Creating the account and verifying it by e-mail;
  • Managing access rights, devices, invitations and subscriptions;
  • Receiving and processing commands and performing security checks;
  • Recording payment transactions and investigating requests and complaints;
  • Delivering in-app and push notifications;
  • Detecting technical faults and improving the service.

5. Payment data

Payments for Salam Sesame are made through the mobile app. Payments may be processed using the infrastructure of a bank or payment provider. Card saving and auto-renewal apply only when the relevant integration is active and with the consent of the user. Full card details are not intended to be stored on Salam Sesame servers.

6. Retention and data security

The main servers are located in the Republic of Azerbaijan. Data is retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, verify user permissions, ensure security and fulfil legal obligations. The retention period and deletion rules for activity history are to be governed by a separate internal policy.

Access to data is restricted according to authorization, and appropriate technical security measures are applied.

7. Third-party services

Google Firebase is intended to be used for delivering push notifications. In this connection, notification tokens and technical data may be processed through Google infrastructure. Data necessary for executing payments is shared with the integrated bank and payment provider.

To keep the app stable, Google Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports: app crashes, the device model, the operating system version and an internal user number. Firebase Analytics measures how the app is used (screens opened and key actions) as statistics. This data is not used for advertising and is not sold; usage statistics can be turned off in the app under Settings → Privacy. Address search and the map use the GoMap.az service through our servers: the searched address and the point picked on the map are sent to GoMap, never the user's name or contact details.

8. Information shown to other users

Administrators authorized for the relevant device and complex may see the first name, last name and e-mail address of users for the purpose of managing access permissions. The extent of user data that is visible depends on the relevant role and permissions.

9. Notifications and usage history

The app may send push and in-app notifications about subscription, invitation, device and other service events. Whether notifications are displayed also depends on the operating system settings of the device. Access and activity events may be recorded for service security and for the investigation of disputes.

10. Account deactivation and data deletion

Deactivating an account and fully deleting personal data are separate operations. When an account is deactivated, the account data and any active subscription may be retained and notifications may be stopped; the user can restore use of the account by signing in again.

A separate request may be submitted for the deletion of personal data. Such requests are carried out in accordance with legal requirements and with regard to data that must be retained by law. A change in the technical status of an account is not presented as the automatic deletion of all data.

11. User rights

In accordance with the law, the user has the right to obtain information about their data, to have inaccurate data corrected, to request its deletion where applicable, and to submit requests.

12. Changes and contact

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service or the law changes. For requests, you may use the Contact section of the official website.

1. About the service

Salam Sesame is a service and device solution provided by Alkin Group MMC that allows users to send commands from a mobile app to compatible automatic access devices and to manage access rights.

2. Registration and account

The user creates an account with their first name, last name, phone number and e-mail address. Verification with a one-time code sent by e-mail may be required. Completing registration does not in itself create any device or access authorization.

3. Devices and access rights

Salam Sesame may be used for private devices and for devices belonging to residential complexes. Access rights are granted on the basis of authorization for the relevant device and the terms of an active subscription. A single user may have different roles on different devices.

4. Building manager and other users

An authorized building manager of a residential complex may invite residents and manage the relevant access rights. An authorized subscriber may add family members. The access rights of family members and other users are determined in accordance with their separate authorization and subscription terms.

5. Guest access

Where the feature is active, an authorized user may create a one-time or time-limited guest access link. The user is responsible for ensuring that the link is not shared with unauthorized persons.

6. Subscription and payment

Salam Sesame is provided under an annual subscription model. Rates are determined according to the request, the customer category and the agreed commercial terms. Subscription payments are made through the mobile app. Auto-renewal applies only with the separate consent of the user and when the relevant feature is active.

7. Sending commands

The mobile app transmits commands to the access automation through a compatible device. The user acknowledges that the internet connection and power supply of the device, as well as the operation of the access mechanism, may affect the result. Acceptance of a command does not always confirm that the physical gate has actually opened or closed.

8. Rules of use and restrictions

The user must not make unauthorized use of the account of another person, compromise the security system, share links for unlawful purposes or use access devices in a dangerous manner.

9. Account management

The user may submit a request through the appropriate channels to deactivate the account or delete data. Upon deactivation, an active subscription may be retained and the account may be reactivated. Full deletion of personal data is carried out under a separate procedure.

10. Support and requests

Requests concerning the service, installation or payment may be submitted through the help section of the mobile app and the Contact section of the website.

11. Changes to the terms

The current version of these terms is published on the official Salam Sesame website. Where required by law, users may be given appropriate notice of changes.

This policy governs cancellation and refund requests relating to services and subscriptions paid for through the Salam Sesame mobile app and to devices provided by Alkin Group MMC.

The price of devices, installation and services is determined after a request and individual negotiations. The commercial terms offered to the customer are stated in the relevant quote, and the subscription terms are shown in the mobile app before payment.

3. Subscription period and auto-renewal

The Salam Sesame subscription model is intended to be on an annual basis. A user stopping subscription renewal at their own request does not mean that the previous payment is automatically refunded. Refund rights provided by law are preserved. The user may contact support for a refund if they cancel the service immediately after payment, if a payment was made in error or more than once, or if they have a substantiated complaint about the service.

Requests are reviewed individually, taking into account the transaction details, the status of service delivery, the agreed terms and the applicable law. The user is informed of the refund decision.

The warranty period for Salam Sesame devices is 12 months. In the event of technical and manufacturing defects covered by the warranty terms, repair or replacement of the device is provided. Other consumer rights provided by law remain in force.

Account deactivation

A user may contact support to have their account temporarily deactivated. Deactivation does not automatically cancel the account data or an active subscription. The user can restore use of their account by signing in again with the same e-mail.

Stopping notifications

A deactivation request includes stopping notifications. This does not mean that access permissions or the subscription are automatically deleted.

Account deletion

Account deletion is different from deactivation: a deleted account cannot be restored.

From the mobile app

In the Salam Sesame app, open Profile → Delete account and confirm the deletion. The account is deleted immediately.

If you cannot sign in to the app

From the e-mail address you registered with, write to support@sezam.salam.az with the subject “Account deletion”. We process the request after confirming that you are the account owner.

What is deleted

  • First name, last name, phone number and e-mail address;
  • Notification (push) data and the last sign-in IP address;
  • All device access permissions — access stops immediately;
  • Active subscriptions are cancelled (no refund is given for the remaining period);
  • Access granted to family members through you is also revoked.

What is retained

Payment and transaction records are kept without personal data for the period required by law. The security log retains only an irreversible encrypted trace of the phone number and e-mail address.

Device owners

If you are the owner of a device, you must transfer ownership to another user before deleting your account.

After deletion, you can register again with the same phone number and e-mail.

How to submit a request

For deactivation and deletion requests, write to support@sezam.salam.az or use the Contact page of the website. In your request, state your account e-mail address and the type of request.
